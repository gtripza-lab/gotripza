## Travel insurance is about risk, not fear

Insurance becomes more important when the trip is expensive, far from home, involves multiple flights, includes older travelers, or depends on non-refundable bookings.

## What to check

| Coverage | Why it matters |
|----------|----------------|
| Medical expenses | Healthcare abroad can be expensive |
| Trip cancellation | Protects prepaid non-refundable costs |
| Delays | Helps with long disruptions |
| Lost luggage | Useful when bags contain essentials |
| Adventure exclusions | Important for skiing, diving, hiking |

## Common mistakes

Many travelers buy a policy without checking exclusions. Read medical limits, pre-existing condition rules, destination coverage, and whether your planned activities are included.
