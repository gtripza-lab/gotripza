## Start with the real cost categories

A useful travel budget is more than flights and hotels. It should include local transport, food, activities, insurance, mobile data, visa fees, airport transfers, and a small emergency buffer.

## Basic trip budget structure

| Category | What to include |
|----------|-----------------|
| Flights | Bags, seat selection, airport timing |
| Stay | Taxes, resort fees, location quality |
| Food | Breakfast, daily meals, coffee, snacks |
| Transport | Metro, taxis, ride apps, transfers |
| Activities | Tickets, tours, guides, tips |
| Safety buffer | Insurance, emergency cash, delays |

## A good rule

After estimating the main costs, add a 10-20% buffer. Travel rarely goes exactly as planned, and the buffer keeps the trip calm.
