## Short answer

The best time to visit Dubai is usually **November to March**. The weather is comfortable, outdoor attractions are easier, and beach days feel pleasant. Prices are higher during this period, especially around New Year and major events.

## Dubai by season

| Season | Weather | Best for |
|--------|---------|----------|
| Nov-Mar | Mild and pleasant | Families, beaches, outdoor dining |
| Apr-May | Warm | Better hotel value, short trips |
| Jun-Sep | Very hot | Indoor attractions, lower prices |
| Oct | Improving weather | Good balance before peak season |

## When should families go?

Families usually enjoy December to February the most because walking, parks, beaches, and outdoor malls are easier. Book early if you travel during school holidays.

## When is Dubai cheapest?

Summer can be cheaper for hotels, but the heat changes the trip style. Plan more indoor activities, taxis, malls, aquariums, museums, and evening outings.
