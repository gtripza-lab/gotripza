## Activities should match the trip, not fill every hour

The best travel activities are the ones that fit your energy, budget, weather, and group. A good itinerary leaves room for rest, meals, delays, and spontaneous discoveries.

## Match activities to travel style

| Travel style | Good activity types |
|--------------|---------------------|
| Family | Short guided tours, aquariums, parks, easy food stops |
| Couple | Sunset views, private experiences, relaxed dining |
| Solo | Walking tours, museums, local food, safe group trips |
| Adventure | Hiking, water activities, desert or mountain tours |

## Avoid overbooking

Two major activities in one day can be enough. If the destination has traffic, heat, or long transfers, one focused activity plus a relaxed evening is often better.

## Questions to ask before booking

1. Is pickup included?
2. What is the cancellation policy?
3. Is it suitable for children or older travelers?
4. Does the timing work with weather and traffic?
